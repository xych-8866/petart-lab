At the 28th Asia Pet Fair in Shanghai, a wave of smart technology swept through the exhibition halls — cat face-recognition feeders, real-time heart rate monitoring smart harnesses, and remote-controlled companion robots[reference:28]. The message was clear: pet technology is moving from "geek exotica" into the mass market, and it's transforming how we care for our pets[reference:29].

📊 Market Trend: Global pet wearable market CAGR is 14.3%, projected to reach $6.9 billion by 2030[reference:30]. In 2025, China's urban pet (dogs and cats) consumption market reached RMB 312.6 billion (approximately $43 billion), up 4.1% year-over-year[reference:31].

1. Smart Feeders: Cat Face Recognition & AI-Powered Nutrition

One of the standout technologies at Pet Fair Asia 2026 was the cat face-recognition smart feeder[reference:32]. These devices can identify individual cats in multi-cat households, control portion sizes, and track eating patterns. PETKIT also showcased their smart AI wet food feeder and smart water fountain ULTRA, which collect daily behavior data and generate visual health reports using large language models[reference:33].

2. Smart Health Monitoring: AI Collars and Harnesses

Several companies showcased wearable technology for pets at the event[reference:34]:

  • Smart Harnesses: Equipped with sensors that collect real-time heart rate, temperature, and activity data, and use large language models to assess health status, emotional state, and even heatstroke risk[reference:35]
  • AI Health Collars: China Mobile unveiled an AI smart visual pet collar with six core functions: meter-level precise GPS positioning, two-way voice calling, HD video interaction, AI health monitoring, 1:1 digital avatar companionship, and smart light pet tracking[reference:36]
  • Petsense™ AI: SATELLAI's multi-modal pet health software platform uses AI models to analyze pet health data[reference:37]

3. Emotional AI: Pet Translators and AI Diaries

Pet technology is increasingly focused on emotional connection[reference:38]:

  • PettiChat (萌小译): The world's first AI pet real-time translation hardware, launched in April 2026. Weighing just 27.2g, it clips onto a pet's collar and translates vocalizations in real-time[reference:39]. It achieves 94.6% accuracy for cats and approximately 92.3% for dogs in emotion and situation recognition[reference:40].
  • PurrPurr: An AI emotional companion collar weighing just 17.9g — the lightest in the world. It writes an "AI diary" for your pet in the first person, capturing their daily moods and stories[reference:41]. It also creates MBTI personality profiles for pets[reference:42].
  • Remote-controlled companion robots: Allowing owners to interact with their pets even when they're away from home[reference:43]

4. Smart Litter Boxes and Health Monitoring

Smart litter boxes are evolving from simple automatic cleaning to comprehensive health monitoring. PETKIT's smart automatic litter box ULTRA and other products at the fair collect data on elimination patterns, which can help detect early signs of urinary issues, kidney disease, and other conditions[reference:44]. As one attendee noted, "These devices can remind you of abnormalities in time, which is very meaningful"[reference:45].
