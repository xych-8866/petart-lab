2. PettiChat (萌小译): The Real-Time Pet Translator

PettiChat is the world's first AI pet real-time translation hardware, launched in April 2026[reference:24]. Weighing just 27.2g, it clips onto your pet's collar and translates vocalizations into human language in real-time[reference:25].

  • Cat accuracy: 94.6% in emotion and situation recognition[reference:26]
  • Dog accuracy: Approximately 92.3%
  • Emotions translated: 20 categories for cats, 10 categories for dogs — including hunger, playfulness, fear, and discomfort[reference:27]
  • Orders: 20,000+ units sold in the first month and a half, covering 30+ countries[reference:28]
  • Development: 2 years of R&D with Zhejiang University's School of Animal Science, 5+ million animal behavior data points, and Alibaba Cloud's Tongyi Qianwen large language model[reference:29]

As one visitor at the Asia Pet Fair experienced: "I used the AI pet translator to input a cat's meow, and it instantly translated it into human language: 'Meow, don't procrastinate, meow is bored right now.'"[reference:30]
