1. The "Glass Stomach" Epidemic

Digestive sensitivity — often called "玻璃胃" (glass stomach) — is one of the most common health concerns in dogs and cats. As pets have moved indoors and diets have become more processed, the incidence of digestive issues has skyrocketed. According to industry data, digestive health has become the leading consumer focus in pet nutrition, with pet owners increasingly seeking solutions for sensitive stomachs[reference:3].

2. The Gut-Brain Axis: Why Gut Health Affects Behavior

The gut-brain axis is the bidirectional communication network between the gastrointestinal tract and the brain[reference:4]. This means that gut health doesn't just affect digestion — it affects behavior, mood, and even cognitive function.

  • Anxiety and stress: An unhealthy gut can contribute to anxiety, destructive behavior, and irritability
  • Immune function: Up to 70% of the immune system resides in the gut
  • Skin health: Gut inflammation often manifests as skin issues
  • Cognitive function: The gut microbiome influences brain health and cognitive aging

TypeWhat It DoesExamples
ProbioticsLive beneficial bacteria that colonize the gutEnterococcus faecium, Bifidobacterium, Lactobacillus
PrebioticsFiber that feeds beneficial bacteriaBaobab fruit pulp, acacia gum, inulin, FOS[reference:5]
PostbioticsInactivated bacteria and beneficial compounds produced by probioticsInactivated L. helveticus, yeast fractions[reference:6]

In 2026, postbiotics have emerged as a major trend in pet gut health[reference:7]. Unlike probiotics (which must survive digestion), postbiotics are already inactivated, providing the benefits without the viability concerns[reference:8].

4. Signs Your Pet Needs Gut Support

  • Chronic diarrhea or soft stools — the most common sign
  • Excessive gas or flatulence
  • Poor coat quality — dull, dry, or excessive shedding
  • Skin issues — itching, hot spots, or recurrent ear infections
  • Behavioral changes — anxiety, irritability, or decreased activity
  • Recurrent infections — frequent ear, skin, or urinary infections
  • Food sensitivities — reactions to multiple foods
⚠️ If your pet has multiple signs, consult your vet. Gut health issues can indicate underlying conditions that require professional attention.
