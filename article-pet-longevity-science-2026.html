Joint Health

Joint health remains the foundation of pet longevity. As pets age, maintaining mobility is essential for quality of life. Key interventions include:

  • Joint supplements: Glucosamine, chondroitin, MSM, and green-lipped mussel
  • Weight management: Every pound of excess weight adds strain to joints
  • Low-impact exercise: Swimming, gentle walks, and physical therapy
  • New medications: Librela and Adequan for pain management

The Microbiome

The gut microbiome is emerging as a critical factor in overall health and longevity. A healthy microbiome supports immunity, digestion, and even brain health[reference:7]. The rise of synbiotics (probiotics + prebiotics) represents the next frontier in digestive health[reference:8].
