4. How to Choose the Right Toy

  • Consider your pet's play style: Chase, fetch, or puzzle solving?
  • Consider your pet's size: Some toys are not suitable for large dogs
  • Consider your pet's intelligence: Smart pets need more challenging toys
  • Consider the features: Do you need remote control, treat dispensing, or both?
  • Consider your budget: Prices range from $30 to $200

6. What to Avoid

  • Poor-quality materials — can break easily
  • Short battery life — frequent charging is inconvenient
  • Toys that are too loud — can scare anxious pets
  • Toys that are too simple — smart pets will get bored quickly

  3. Introduce the toy gradually with positive reinforcement
  4. Use the toy to provide mental stimulation during alone time
