4. How to Choose the Right Wipes

  • Consider the purpose: All-purpose, paw, ear, or eye – choose accordingly
  • Consider your pet's sensitivity: Choose hypoallergenic and fragrance-free for sensitive pets
  • Consider the ingredients: Look for natural ingredients (aloe, vitamin E) and avoid harsh chemicals
  • Consider packaging: Choose resealable packs to keep wipes moist

5. How to Use Pet Wipes Safely

  1. Read the product label for specific instructions
  2. Avoid contact with eyes (unless using eye wipes)
  3. Use a fresh wipe for each area or pet
  4. Do not use on open wounds or irritated skin
  5. Dispose of used wipes properly (do not flush)

6. Real Owner Success Story

7. What to Avoid

  • Wipes with harsh chemicals – can cause skin irritation
  • Non-flushable wipes – flushable wipes can cause plumbing problems
  • Using the same wipe for multiple areas – can spread bacteria
  • Using expired wipes – may not be effective

8. Your Action Plan

  2. Choose wipes that match your needs and your pet's sensitivity
  3. Keep wipes in convenient locations (car, home, bag)
  4. Use wipes daily or as needed for hygiene and comfort
