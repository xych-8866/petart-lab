📊 Fact: Temperatures above 85°F can cause heat stress in dogs. Cooling products help regulate body temperature and prevent heat-related illnesses like heatstroke. The right product can make summer safer and more comfortable for your pet.

1. Why Cooling Products Matter

  • Prevents heatstroke – dangerous body temperature spike
  • Reduces discomfort – keeps pets cool during hot weather
  • Improves mobility – arthritic pets benefit from cooler temperatures
  • Enables outdoor activities – longer walks and hikes in summer
  • Protects brachycephalic breeds – breeds at higher risk of overheating

4. Other Summer Safety Tips

  • Hydration: Always carry water on walks
  • Time walks: Walk early morning or late evening
  • Check pavement: If it's too hot for your hand, it's too hot for your pet's paws
  • Never leave pets in cars: Even with windows cracked, temperatures can rise quickly
  • Provide shade: Ensure outdoor areas have adequate shade
  • Watch for signs of heatstroke: Panting, drooling, red gums, weakness

6. What to Avoid

  • Cooling mats that are too cold – can cause discomfort if too cold
  • Non-breathable materials – can cause skin irritation
  • Cheap, low-quality vests – may not provide adequate cooling
  • Not cleaning cooling products – can harbor bacteria and odor

7. Your Action Plan

  1. Choose a cooling product that fits your pet's size and lifestyle
  2. Keep water available at all times during summer
  3. Monitor for signs of heat stress and adjust activity levels
  4. Store cooling products properly and clean them regularly
