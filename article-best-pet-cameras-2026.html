4. How to Choose the Right Pet Camera

  • Consider your pet's needs: Do they need treat interaction, or just monitoring?
  • Consider your home layout: Pan/tilt features are useful for larger rooms
  • Consider your budget: Prices range from $80 to $300+
  • Consider subscription requirements: Some features may require a paid plan[reference:38]
  • Consider treat capacity: If you plan to use treat dispensing often, choose a larger capacity[reference:39]
