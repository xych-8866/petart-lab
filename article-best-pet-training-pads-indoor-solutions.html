1. Why Use Training Pads?

  • Consistency: Provides a consistent elimination spot
  • Damage control: Protects floors and carpets from accidents
  • Senior pets: Useful for dogs with mobility issues or incontinence
  • Apartment dwellers: Convenient alternative for late-night potty breaks
  • Weather protection: When it's too hot, cold, or rainy to go outside

3. What to Look For

  • Scent-attracting pheromones: Encourages your pet to use the pad
  • Leak-proof backing: Prevents liquid from seeping onto floors
  • Super absorbent core: Locks in moisture and reduces odor
  • Size: Choose pads that are large enough for your dog
  • Thickness: More layers = better absorption
  • Multi-pack value: Bulk packs are more economical

5. How to Train Your Puppy to Use Pads

  1. Place the pad in a designated area (quiet, accessible, not near food or water)
  2. Take your puppy to the pad frequently (after meals, playtime, waking up)
  3. Reward success with praise and treats
  4. Gradually reduce the number of pads to encourage outdoor elimination
  5. Transition to outdoor potty by moving the pad closer to the door

7. What to Avoid

  • Placing pads too far from resting area – they won't have time to reach it
  • Using scented pads (strong fragrance) – may discourage elimination
  • Not replacing pads frequently – soiled pads lose attractant properties
  • Expecting perfection too soon – accidents are part of the process

8. Your Action Plan

  2. Set up the pad in a designated area
  3. Use positive reinforcement to train your pet
  4. Gradually transition to outdoor elimination (if applicable)
  5. Change pads regularly and clean the area
