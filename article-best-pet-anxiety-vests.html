1. How Anxiety Vests Work

Anxiety vests (also called calming wraps or Thundershirts) use pressure therapy to provide a gentle, constant pressure on your pet's body. This mimics the calming effect of swaddling in infants, releasing endorphins and reducing stress hormones.

  • Deep pressure stimulation – calms the nervous system
  • Reduces cortisol – lowers stress hormone levels
  • Increases oxytocin – promotes bonding and relaxation
  • No side effects – non-pharmacological intervention

2. When to Use an Anxiety Vest

  • Thunderstorms and fireworks – most common use
  • Travel anxiety – car rides, flights, and vet visits
  • Separation anxiety – when left alone
  • Grooming and vet visits – reduces stress during procedures
  • General anxiety – for pets with chronic anxiety

4. How to Fit an Anxiety Vest

  1. Measure your pet's chest girth (around the widest part)
  2. Check the manufacturer's size chart
  3. Ensure the vest fits snugly but not too tight
  4. You should be able to fit two fingers under the vest
  5. Adjust straps for a comfortable fit

5. How to Train Your Pet to Wear an Anxiety Vest

  1. Introduce the vest without fastening it – let your pet sniff it
  2. Place the vest on loosely for a few minutes, reward with treats
  3. Gradually increase the time your pet wears the vest
  4. Associate the vest with positive experiences (treats, playtime)
  5. Use the vest during stressful events for best results

7. What to Avoid

  • Vests that are too tight – can restrict breathing
  • Vests that are too loose – won't provide pressure therapy
  • Using the vest as a punishment – should be a positive experience
  • Relying only on the vest – combine with behavior modification
