1. What Is a Snuffle Mat?

A snuffle mat is a sensory enrichment tool made of fabric strips (felt or fleece) attached to a durable base. Treats or kibble are hidden between the strips, and your pet must use their nose to find and eat the food. This mimics natural foraging behaviors and provides both mental and physical stimulation.

2. Benefits of Snuffle Mats

  • Mental stimulation: Engages your pet's brain and senses
  • Slow feeding: Slows down eating, reducing the risk of bloat
  • Anxiety reduction: The act of sniffing is calming and releases endorphins
  • Boredom relief: Prevents destructive behaviors
  • Training tool: Helps with impulse control and focus

5. How to Use a Snuffle Mat

  1. Place the mat on a flat, stable surface
  2. Hide kibble, treats, or even vegetables between the fabric strips
  3. Let your pet sniff and find the food
  4. Supervise to ensure your pet doesn't chew the fabric
  5. Wash the mat regularly (machine washable)

7. What to Avoid

  • Using too much food – can make it overwhelming
  • Leaving the mat unattended – some pets may chew the fabric
  • Using wet food – can make the mat sticky and hard to clean
  • Not cleaning the mat regularly – can harbor bacteria
